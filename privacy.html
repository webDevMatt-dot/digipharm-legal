Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

DigiPharm is an online health and wellness shop operated by Matthew Luis in South Africa ("DigiPharm", "we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information the DigiPharm app collects, why, who it is shared with and the rights you have under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Information we collect

Account details: your name, email address, mobile number and password. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider in hashed form; we never see them.

Delivery details: the address, delivery window and any notes you enter at checkout.

Orders: the products you buy, quantities, prices, payment method and order status.

Favourites: products you save to your account.

Your cart is stored only on your device until you place an order.

You can browse the shop without an account. We do not collect personal information from guests.

What we don't collect

We do not use advertising or tracking tools, we do not build profiles of you for marketing, and we do not sell or rent your information.

The app does not collect card details. Orders are currently paid on delivery.

Why we use it

To create and secure your account, process and deliver your orders, contact you about an order, keep the records we are required to keep by law, and answer your questions.

We process your information because it is needed to carry out our agreement with you when you shop with us, to meet legal obligations, or for our legitimate interest in running a safe and reliable service.

Who we share it with

Supabase, which hosts our database, sign-in and file storage. Your data is stored on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Personal information sent outside South Africa is protected under data protection laws that provide protection substantially similar to POPIA, as section 72 of POPIA requires.

The person who delivers your order sees your name, phone number, address and order.

Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not share your information with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Your account information is kept until you delete your account.

Order records are kept for at least five years because South African tax law requires it. When you delete your account, your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and order notes are removed from those records.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Account, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your login, profile and favourites, and removes your personal details from past orders. If an order is still in progress, finish or cancel it first.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. You can update most details yourself in the app under Account.

To make a request, email us at the address below. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted so that customers can only see their own information and only our pharmacist staff can manage orders.

Children

DigiPharm is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant. If we add new services, such as prescription delivery, we will update this policy before they go live.
